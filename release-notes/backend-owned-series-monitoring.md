Monitor Series can request ongoing ebook or audiobook monitoring through a compatible Chaptarr server. Chaptarr handles current and future catalog books and download timing. Requests can await approval, and managers can approve, retry or stop watches from Series Monitoring Requests. Request Selected Books remains available for individual selections.
