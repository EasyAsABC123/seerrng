Administrators can enable Kobo delivery for a BookOrbit live shelf. SeerrNG
keeps the SmartScope for browsing and maintains a matching native BookOrbit
Collection for Kobo, refreshing its membership every 15 minutes by default.
The sync schedule can be adjusted under Settings > Jobs. BookOrbit still needs
a Kobo account paired, and the managed Collection is reserved for SeerrNG.
